## Overview

<span className="highlighted">Think Thieves</span> is a re-imagining of the official 100 Thieves
website, designed to place a greater emphasis on the organization's
<span className="highlighted">esports teams and content creators</span>. While the original site
focuses heavily on the broader 100 Thieves brand, I wanted to create an experience that put the
organization's competitive teams and live content at the forefront.

The application provides users with information about upcoming esports matches and currently live
streams in one centralized location. Match data is retrieved for 100 Thieves'
<span className="highlighted">VALORANT, League of Legends, and Call of Duty League</span> teams,
while live streams from 100 Thieves creators are surfaced from both Twitch and YouTube.

To keep the displayed information current, <span className="highlighted">web scraping and APIs</span>
are used together. VALORANT match data is collected by scraping VLR.gg, while League of Legends
and Call of Duty League match data is retrieved through the Liquipedia API. Live Twitch streams
are retrieved through the Twitch API, while YouTube streams are identified by scraping the channels
of creators who stream on the platform.

Because these data sources can be queried frequently, <span className="highlighted">Redis</span>
is used to cache match and stream data and prevent unnecessary API requests and web scrapes. The
Puppeteer-based scraper runs through an <span className="highlighted">Express</span> backend,
while the frontend was built with <span className="highlighted">React</span>.

Building Think Thieves gave me hands-on experience working with external APIs, web scraping,
caching strategies, and combining multiple data sources into a single application. It also
strengthened my understanding of building a frontend around dynamic, frequently changing data
rather than relying solely on a static backend dataset.

The project is a personal re-imagining rather than an official 100 Thieves product, and much of
the imagery and copywriting was borrowed from the organization's official website for the purposes
of the project.
